## What it says

1. **Multiple-scattering default (K6.3).** Over 12 scenes 1D diffusion (`true`, the design's incumbent) is the weakest
   energy model wherever multiple scattering matters (−18 % thin to −71 % WDAS), and it is **exactly single scattering
   at albedo 0.5** (its depth `min(direct, diffusion)` never picks diffusion in an absorbing medium, where the
   reference carries +70 % over single scattering). Octaves overshoot thin and absorbing media (+36 %, +63 %) and
   undershoot thick ones. Neural and two-sided are the best pair: neural has the lowest (or tied) median error in 7 of 12 scenes
   (13–32 %; ≤ 10 % on thin media), two-sided the best energy on his bunny (+2 %) and thin media but +22–30 % on the
   smoke and the point light. The design's choice is diffusion vs multi-scale; multi-scale (K7.3) has a slot row in
   every scene group and should be ranked against the neural / two-sided rows. Recommendation: do not keep diffusion
   as the default.
2. **Ambient light (V85)**: with ambient every renderer mode is too bright against the reference's single scattering
   (+35 % thin, +100–148 % thick): the renderer's ambient is not shadowed. On the absorbing bunny + ambient that error
   dominates and ms off beats every MS mode. A shadowed ambient is the fix, not an MS mode.
3. **Single scattering** is exact on the thin bunny (≤ 1 %); 'volume' lighting is within −13 to +4 % on thick media;
   'frame' lighting −22 to −41 % on his thick bunnies (the 64³ shadow grid) and +22 % on the WDAS cloud.
4. **V79**: 'mixture' and 'over' differ by ≤ 0.1 points everywhere, also in the overlap region; both are a little
   closer than the TSL draw order (−3.6 / −3.7 % vs −4.3 % in the overlap, below the 3 % reference noise). Mixing is
   not a quality question here; 'mixture' stays for order independence.
5. **V80**: the core's prefix filtering is closer to the reference at silhouettes than his per-slice composite (52.5 %
   vs 56.3 % at 640 × 360, lower in every row at 160 × 90). K6.1b is not needed for quality.
6. **V96**: core 'outward' (SH L2) and TSL 'outward' (PMREM) are within 2 points against the MS reference (7 against
   SS, inside that reference's noise); both are ~+80 % too bright against single scattering (the outward term does not
   see the volume shadow itself), which dwarfs SH vs PMREM. Keep SH L2; a sharp verdict needs environment NEE.
7. **Light mapping**: with each mode's own mapping the point-light scene's single scattering is −13 % ('volume') and
   −7.6 % ('frame'), in the range of the directional scenes: the report measures approximation, not convention.
8. **Core = TSL** to ≤ 0.2 points of bias in every comparable row, with slightly better single scattering on his bunny
   (−5.9 vs −7.9 % 'volume', −21.9 vs −23.7 % 'frame').
