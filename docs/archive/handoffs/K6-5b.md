# K6.5b handoff: mesh → volume shadows (host occluder maps) in the core and `./three`

Design: [K6-core-renderer.md](../../dev/design/K6-core-renderer.md) §2.3 (`VDBFrameOccluder` as built), §3.2 (sampled
textures ≤ 16), §3.7 (now with a "K6.5b as built" note), D34. Builds on [K6-5.md](./K6-5.md) (its K6.5b scope list),
[K6-2.md](./K6-2.md) (the 'frame' transmittance and fused gather) and [K6-2b.md](./K6-2b.md).

**Landed:** K6.5b whole. Meshes shadow volumes: the host's depth shadow maps stop the light in the 'frame' transmittance
(scattered part), the fused 'frame' gather (direct part) and the 'volume' march (whole term), for directional, spot and
point lights. `settings.occlusion` (default true). CPU twins with fail-before mutations. `./three` (`backend: 'core'`)
passes three's light shadow maps, self-tested, **with no lag** (three draws its pending maps before the core update).

## Method (why this one)

His renderer has no mesh → volume shadows. Native volumetric fog does it by sampling the light's shadow map at each
froxel (Hillaire 2015, Frostbite; Unreal's volumetric fog light-scattering pass with the light's shadow maps): a march
from a point toward the light stops at the first occluder, which is the map test at the point. The core does exactly
that at the position each kernel lights:

| Kernel | Where V is evaluated | What it multiplies |
|---|---|---|
| 'frame' transmittance (grid voxel) | voxel centre | the slot's scattered part: stores `direct + (total − direct)·V` (direct stays unoccluded) |
| 'frame' fused gather, density part (per volume) | the volume's visible position in the froxel | each slot's direct: `phase·(direct·V) + (total − direct)` |
| 'frame' fused gather, lighting part | the summed visible position | lights without a slot (`phase·V`), and the per-channel phase difference of slot lights |
| 'volume' gather | the lit (visible) position | the light's whole term (spot attenuation × V; the march is skipped at V = 0) |

Separation of terms (design §3.7): light diffusing into a mesh shadow (the 1D-diffusion scattered part) is smooth at grid
resolution, the direct light is sharp at froxel resolution. 'volume' lighting has no grid, so both terms take V at the
lit position. Sky, hemisphere, ambient, probe and environment light are not occluded (no host map for them).

**Lookup** (`core/frame/wgsl/occluders.ts`, twin `tools/frame/occluders.ts` `vdbFrameOccluderVisibility`): the first
layer whose clip volume holds the position (`|x|,|y| ≤ 1`, `0 ≤ z ≤ 1`, `w > 0`), so cascades (finest first) and cube
faces need no kind-specific code; reference `z + bias` (reversed: `z − bias`; three's sign), 2 × 2 PCF with exact
bilinear weights (three's hardware comparison filter) or one texel (`'nearest'`, three's `BasicShadowMap`); outside
every layer: lit (three's frustum rule).

**Bindings** (the K6.5 handoff's limits question): 4 fixed slots at group-1 bindings 12–15 of the transmittance and both
gathers, `texture_2d_array<f32>` `unfilterable-float` read with `textureLoad`, a 1 × 1 r32float placeholder in empty
slots. Not a comparison sampler: one binding type takes depth and r32float maps (a comparison sampler needs the `depth`
sample type), reversed depth is a uniform flag (not a second sampler), and the twin matches exactly (hardware PCF
quantizes its weights). The data lives in `VdbFrameLights` (no new uniform buffer: the dense 'frame' gather is at 5):
`counts.y` maps bound (0: occlusion off), `falloff.z` = slot + 1 per light, `occluderInfo[4]` (layers, bias, reversed,
nearest), `occluderMatrix[24]` (6 layers per slot). Sampled textures: the worst kernel (dense 'frame' gather, every
channel with mips) goes from 11 to 15; the limits test's cap is now 16, WebGPU's default `maxSampledTexturesPerShaderStage`
(compat keeps 16), and a new test pins the worst at 15 so the next addition is a decision. A "depth 2d-array per kind"
cannot hold host textures without copying them.

**No occluder → no change**: every factor is an exact `× 1.0` and the transmittance's branch (`V < 1`) is not taken, so
frames without maps are byte-identical to main (checked, below). A map without `revision` redraws the transmittance every
update; with one, the transmittance signature covers the slot lights' views, matrices, bias, flags and revisions.

## Design changes (please review)

1. **`textureLoad` + manual 2 × 2 PCF instead of a comparison sampler** (design said "a comparison sampler"; reasons
   above). `VDBFrameOccluder` loses `orthographic` (the matrix says it) and gains `filter` and `revision`; the matrices
   are `ArrayLike<number>[]` and the view must be `'2d-array'`.
2. **The sampled-texture cap goes 12 → 16** (the WebGPU default), not a rework: the worst kernel is 15; pinned by a test.
3. **Occluder data in `VdbFrameLights`**, not a new uniform (the dense 'frame' gather is at the 5-uniform cap of the
   limits test). Bindings 12–15 everywhere (other passes keep the low numbers).
4. **The layer is chosen by containment**, not by view-depth breaks: hosts pass cascades finest first; point maps are 6
   layers with their own matrices (no cube convention in the core).
5. **`./three` removes the one-render lag** by calling the light's `ShadowNode.updateBefore` (three r187's own per-frame
   guard then skips the map later in the frame), instead of documenting a lag. Matrices are read from the shadow camera /
   `shadow.matrix` after that call, so map and matrices always match, also for maps three does not redraw.
6. The transmittance redraws every update while a slot light has a map without `revision` (the adapter gives one: three
   redraws `autoUpdate` maps each frame, so in practice the transmittance redraws whenever a shadowing light exists).

## DIVERGES rows (provisional, V114–V117; his renderer is mjurczyk 75e7345)

- V114 — K6.5b: mesh → volume shadows (his renderer has none): host depth shadow maps stop each light at the first
  occluder — the 'frame' transmittance's scattered part × V(voxel), the fused gather's direct part × V(visible position),
  lights without a slot × V, 'volume' lighting's whole light term × V (march skipped at 0). Method: volumetric fog's
  per-froxel shadow-map lookup (Hillaire 2015 / Frostbite, Unreal).
- V115 — K6.5b: the core reads occluder maps with `textureLoad` and a 2 × 2 bilinear PCF of its own (or one texel), not a
  comparison sampler; three's receivers use a 5-tap Vogel-disk PCF (`PCFShadowMap`) and seamless cube filtering (point),
  so volume shadow edges are filtered over 2 × 2 texels within one face.
- V116 — K6.5b: `./three` draws a light's pending shadow map before the core update (`ShadowNode.updateBefore` from the
  adapter) instead of at the first lit material three draws; three skips it later in that frame. Same map, earlier.
- V117 — K6.5b: not mapped: `shadow.normalBias` (a volume sample has no normal), logarithmic depth buffers (warned,
  occlusion off), `CSMShadowNode` cascades (each is a light of its own to three; follow-up), VSM (its depth texture is
  still a depth map and is read as one).

## Follow-ups

- CSM (`CSMShadowNode`): pass its cascade lights' maps as one light's layers (finest first).
- The scattered part takes V at the voxel centre (smooth at grid resolution); K6.R1's reference should judge whether a
  diffusion-length blur of V (or V at the 2 × 2 × 2 support) is closer for thick clouds (the bunny: extinction 40–100).
- Spectral (K6.4) and K6.3's MS families: the scattered-part occlusion is written against 1D diffusion's total − direct;
  octaves / multi-scale / MLP must apply V to their own scattered term (one line each; flagged to K6.3).
- `prepare()` needs nothing new (no pipeline key); the transmittance redraw policy with `revision`-less maps is a K6.T
  cost question.
- Compat mode: a three cube depth texture made with `textureBindingViewDimension: 'cube'` cannot be viewed as
  `'2d-array'`; the adapter would need a copy there.
