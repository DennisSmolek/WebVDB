## Design changes (please review)

1. **The prefix stores opacity A = 1 − T, not optical depth τ.** Bilinear filtering of the prefix averages what
   it stores; averaging T (and L) is the box filter of the composited image (pixel coverage), averaging τ is not.
   At a silhouette between an opaque and an empty column, his per-slice filtering gives T = Π(1 − ᾱₛ), averaged T
   gives the supersampled answer, averaged τ is further off than both (worked example: his 0.5625, T 0.625, τ 0.5).
   "Zero means empty" still holds (A = 0). f16 keeps T to 2.4e-4 near T = 1 (< 0.1/255); the composite's
   α = 1 − T_b/T_a is coarse only where T is already small. The two-tap formula is otherwise the design's.
2. **`./three` binds core textures through three r187's `ExternalTexture`** (`is3DTexture = true`), not a
   backend-record swap: r187's `createTexture` takes `sourceTexture` as the GPU texture and `destroyTexture` never
   destroys an external texture, so no `destroyTexture` guard is needed. An initialized ExternalTexture cannot be
   re-pointed (three throws "already initialized"), so a new `outputs.version` makes a new stand-in and swaps the
   node's `value`. The once-per-backend self-test (`VDB_ENGINE_MISMATCH`) checks both behaviours.
3. **sRGB blending in `render()` requires `targets.sceneColor`** (a view cannot be copied from; the design's "core copies
   `color`" needs the texture). It throws `VDB_BAD_OPTION` naming the fix.
4. `'over'` across several source groups composites in source-group order (records are grouped), not scene order;
   internal and for the A/B only.
