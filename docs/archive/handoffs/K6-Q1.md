# K6.Q1 handoff: shadowed ambient (`ambientOcclusion`, V85)

Task: TASKS K6.Q1. The K6.R1 report found the renderer's ambient light unshadowed (V85): every ambient scene +35 to
+148 % too bright against single scattering, the largest single-scattering error. Builds on
[K6-R1.md](./K6-R1.md) (report, scenes), [K6-2b.md](./K6-2b.md) (environment, 'outward' SH), [K6-3.md](./K6-3.md)
(MS modes), [K6-5b.md](./K6-5b.md) (occluders). Quality is judged against our reference renderer; no timings (K6.T).

## The method

**Hemispherical visibility from N opposite direction pairs, each the mode's isotropic-light total.** Distant light
(ambient, hemisphere lights, the light probe, and 'frame' lighting's 'outward' environment) is attenuated by
transmittance marched along `ambientOcclusionPairs` pairs (ω_k, −ω_k), ω_k in the hemisphere around the sky direction
(his `frameSkyDirection`: the first hemisphere light's up, else +y). Pair 0 is ± the sky direction; the others sit on a
ring at the equal-area centroid (cos θ = (1 − 1/N)/2), even in azimuth. Each side's value is the lighting mode's
isotropic-light total, the rule his sky up / down slots already use:

- no multiple scattering: the direct transmittance;
- octaves: the octave total of `1/(1+τ)`, **divided by the octave total of an open direction** (1 + Σ bⁿ), so an open
  direction is 1 as in every other mode (V142);
- every other mode (diffusion, two-sided, neural/mlp, multi-scale): the two-sided isotropic diffusion of the pair
  (`frameIsotropicDiffusion`), which passes the white furnace (V_up + V_down = 2 at albedo 1).

The sides average into V_up (the ω_k) and V_down (the −ω_k). The distant light then reads them exactly as his sky up /
down totals: ambient × (V_up + V_down)/2, hemisphere sky × V_up/2 + ground × V_down/2, the probe's SH irradiance along
± sky × V/2, and (in 'frame') the 'outward' environment's `mix(V_down, V_up, …)`.

- **'frame' lighting**: the pairs are marched in the transmittance pass (the shadow grid march, once per grid voxel and
  only when its signature changes); V_up, V_down go into the two spare channels of the fourth target (were constant 1).
  The gather reads them where it read the sky totals: a grid lookup, no march per froxel. Pair 0 reuses the existing
  sky marches, so N pairs add 2(N − 1) grid marches per transmittance voxel.
- **'volume' lighting**: the pairs are items of the gather's one march loop (the kernel's one shadow-march call site),
  through the volume's own shadow march with `ambientOcclusionSteps` steps (0: the volume's `shadowSteps`); the
  hemisphere lights' own sky/ground marches go away (they read the shared V), the unshadowed ambient and 'outward'
  probe irradiance too. 2N marches per froxel per volume with ambient, hemisphere or probe light.

Settings (core `VDBFrameSettings`; `./three` `coreSettings`, so `backend: 'core'` honours them):
`ambientOcclusion` (default **false**), `ambientOcclusionPairs` (default 4, 1–8), `ambientOcclusionSteps` (default 0).
With the setting off every kernel computes what it did before (`select` on a uniform; t3.zw = 1): K6.1–K6.5 probes and
the A/B tests pass unchanged.

### Why this and not the alternatives

- **SH-weighted transmittance / the environment grid**: the env grid is RGB at the last cascade only, drawn only with
  environment lights, and is what K6.4 (spectral) is changing; per-direction T would need a fifth transmittance target
  per cascade (a uniform layout change in the stack K6.4 is also extending). The two spare channels of the fourth
  target carry the hemispherical split, which is all the distant-light terms read (his model is a sky up / down split).
- **Phase-weighted visibility** (∫ p T dω): with g = 0.8 a surface point facing the camera sees ~0.18 by phase vs ~0.5
  isotropic, so I expected the isotropic estimate to stay too bright in single scattering. It does not, measurably: the
  ms-off rows land within a few points of each scene's sun-only error (below). A phase-aware 'frame' variant would also
  make the transmittance view-dependent (a redraw per camera move). Left as a follow-up.
- **Cone tracing through mips** ('volume'): the march's step LOD already coarsens with fewer steps
  (`ambientOcclusionSteps`); the A/B below is that trade.
- **1 pair** (ambient as a hemisphere light along ± sky: no new marches in 'frame') is a reasonable cheap setting
  (A/B below) but 4 is closer on every scene.

Reading it:

1. **Single scattering: fixed.** With ambient, the ms-off rows fall from +35…+148 % to within a few points of each
   scene's own sun-only error (the remaining 'frame' WDAS +45 % / absorbing −17 % are the 64³ shadow grid, as without
   ambient).
2. **Multiple scattering at albedo 1: unchanged, by design.** A non-absorbing medium in uniform light is the white
   furnace: its ambient radiance is exactly the unshadowed term, so the diffusion pairs give V = 1 and nothing moves
   (the MS rows' remaining errors are the sun's MS, K6.R1's finding 1).
3. **Absorbing medium (albedo 0.5) with MS**: the modes that carry real MS energy improve a lot (neural +66 → −10 %,
   two-sided +52 → −23 %, octaves +130 → +56 %); diffusion and multi-scale go from +30 % to −45 %: at albedo 0.5 they are
   single scattering (K6.R1 finding 1), and vs SS they improve from +118 % to −8 %. The overshoot before was the
   unshadowed ambient masking their missing MS energy.
4. **Octaves at albedo 1** get darker (−44 → −55 %, −32 → −48 %): his octave totals are not furnace-preserving. Without
   the normalisation (V142) they were −48 / −38 / +77 / +34 %: mean |bias| 49 % vs 42 % normalised (56 % off).

## Follow-ups

- **Default** (owner / lead): on, 4 pairs, per the numbers above; 'volume' steps (full vs 16) is a K6.T cost question.
- `./three` exposes the setting only through the internal `coreSettings` until K6.8 makes the core the renderer.
- K6.4 (spectral): the pair totals are grey (the luminance albedo in the effective depth, as the sky slots); spectral
  totals per channel would follow the spectral sky slots.
- Phase-weighted ambient (V143) if a scene shows it (e.g. a thin, strongly forward-scattering medium backlit by a sky).
- The probe in 'frame' / AO is his ± sky irradiance (cosine-convolved); the radiance mean per hemisphere (0.767 c₁ vs
  1.023 c₁ on the L1 band) would be closer to the reference's isotropic in-scattering (the WDAS + probe 'frame' row
  stays +58 % vs sun-only +22 %).
- Jitter the ring's azimuth per frame with `jitter` + temporal (smoother with fewer pairs).
- K6.T: the cost of 2(N − 1) extra grid marches per transmittance redraw and 2N 'volume' marches per froxel.
