# K6.2 handoff (with K6.1d): sparse slots in the core renderer, and 'frame' lighting without the environment

Design: [K6-core-renderer.md](../../dev/design/K6-core-renderer.md) §3.3–§3.5 (now with a "K6.2 as built" note),
D34. Builds on [K6-1.md](./K6-1.md) (skeleton) and [K6-7.md](./K6-7.md) (`vdbSparseSourceGpu`).

**Landed:** K6.1d whole; K6.2's shadow grid, transmittance, fused density + lighting gather, multi-group partials, the
`./three` `backend: 'core'` path for 'frame' lighting and sparse sources. **Not landed (K6.2b):** the environment
(`envPrefilter`, `envReduce`, `environmentGrid`, the environment light slot, 'outward' SH irradiance); see Follow-ups.

## Design changes (please review)

1. **The grid uniforms live in `VdbFrameUniforms`**, not a new binding: a sparse 'frame' gather already binds 5
   uniform buffers (frame, lights, volumes, group, the sparse table), and K6.2b's environment buffer makes 6.
2. **Casters in the volume table.** Per source group the records are visible non-casters, visible casters, then
   casters not drawn; the group record's `range` covers the drawn ones and `casters` the casters.
3. **'frame' lighting ignores `froxelMix`**: his density targets are already additive, extinction-weighted sums, so
   overlapping volumes are order-independent in his 'frame' lighting and in the fused gather.
4. **HDDA is a runtime flag** (the volume's `hdda` param) rather than a pipeline key: the skip call is one site, and
   the dense source's `vdbSrcSkipSpan` is one line. Compiled into every march-profile gather.

## DIVERGES rows (provisional, from V90; source mjurczyk 75e7345 unless noted)

- V90 — K6.2: 'frame' lighting fuses the density and lighting passes in one kernel; the density sums stay in registers (no 4 × rgba16float targets) and are summed in f32 before one f16 store (his additive blending rounds each volume to f16) (`FrameDensityMaterial`, `FrameLightingMaterial`).
- V91 — K6.2: the shadow grid and the transmittance are stacked 3D textures read with hardware trilinear, not 2D slice atlases with a manual z lerp; same clamps (`frameGrid.js`, `sampleTiles`).
- V92 — K6.2: the shadow grid is rgba16float even with `texture-formats-tier1` (his RG half float; `.xy` unchanged); the rg16float fast path is not built.
- V93 — K6.2: the shadow casters of the core are the volumes with `castsShadow`, drawn or not; `./three` sets it for every ready volume in the camera's layers (his collector's `casters`).
- V94 — K6.1d: the 'volume' shadow march places sample k at step·(k + noise) also without HDDA (his accumulates `probe += step`); f32 rounding only.

## Traps added to CLAUDE.md

- The k6-core 'frame' probes compare each kernel against its twin fed with the **read-back** output of the kernel
  before it (as K3's harness did); feeding twins with twins compounds f16 rounding and hides which kernel moved.
- Core vs TSL A/Bs need his froxel density (1280 × 720 with 512 × 512 froxels): with ~4 px per froxel the V80
  silhouette band alone exceeds the 0.2 % > 4/255 bar (WDAS at 640 × 360 / 160²: 0.3 %).
