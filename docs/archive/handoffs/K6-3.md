## Design changes (please review)

1. **The Mie table is a uniform buffer, not a LUT texture** (design said group-0 LUT textures): K6.5b used the 16th
   sampled-texture slot's headroom; binary16 keeps it at 16 KiB (compat). ≤ 2^-11 relative vs his f32 texture.
2. **MS resources live in the transmittance kernel's group 2** (the kernel is source-free), so group 1 stays free for
   K6.5b's occluder slots.
3. **The MLP's feature mips are one 3D texture with mips** (not 5 textures, not K7.3's chain: his box filter differs).
4. **`'neural'` is `'mlp'`** with `renderer.mlp` (his weights by default); `./three`'s `neuralNetwork` feeds it.
5. **Octaves under host occluders**: visibility at froxel resolution for both terms (the 1 / (1 + τ) encoding cannot carry
   a voxel visibility); other modes keep K6.5b's voxel-resolution scattered occlusion.

## DIVERGES rows (provisional, from V125; source mjurczyk 75e7345 unless noted)

- V125 — K6.3: the Lorenz-Mie tables in the core are binary16 in a uniform buffer (group 0 binding 4), read with his linear blend; his `getFrameMieMap` is a float32 DataTexture (`frameShaderChunks.js` L191–219). ≤ 2^-11 relative; keeps the 'frame' gather at 15 sampled textures (K6.5b) and fits compat's 16 KiB uniform limit.
- V126 — K6.3: `'neural'` runs in a generic small-MLP evaluator (layer shapes as a uniform, weights in a storage buffer, ≤ 8 layers × 64 wide), EXPERIMENTAL `'mlp'`; his network is compiled into the shader as constants (`frameNeuralChunks.js`; extends V48). Same numbers (unit test, 12 digits).
- V127 — K6.3: the neural feature levels are the mips of one rgba16float 3D texture of side R/2 (≤ 5 mips; a level past the chain reads the last mip), redrawn with the shadow grid; his are five RG half-float z-slice atlases drawn by fragment passes (`FrameGridMipMaterial`, `renderGridMips`). Same box filter, edges and sizes max(1, R >> k).
- V128 — K6.3: `'multi-scale'` (his 4e2d46f, past the pin) wired from K7.3 (V75): the lookup in the transmittance kernel, slot total min(direct + scattered, 1); sky, environment and 'volume' lighting use 1D diffusion (his `isFrameDiffusion`); spectral not yet (K6.4). Core-only settings `multiScaleLevels` (6) and `multiScaleEdge` (`'zero'`; his `'clamp'` for the A/B: ≤ 0.2 points apart in K6.R1's report).
- V129 — K6.3 × K6.5b: host occluder visibility applies to each mode's own total in the transmittance (after two-sided, the MLP, multi-scale); with octaves the transmittance keeps 1 / (1 + τ) and the gather scales the slot's direct and phased octave light by the visibility at the froxel's visible position. Not in his renderer.

## Traps added to CLAUDE.md

- The Lorenz-Mie table is a uniform at group 0 binding 4; hand-built group 0s need it (`mieEntry`).
- K7.3's `mips.record` opens its own pass; the update ends and restarts its pass around it.
- 'mlp' / 'multi-scale' are core-only; `'neural'` in the core is the generic MLP.
- Fail-before mutations of edge-only / far-field terms can pass silently on small probe grids.
- A WGSL local named after a builtin (`length`) shadows it.
