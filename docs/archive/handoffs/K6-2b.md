# K6.2b handoff: the environment on the GPU (envPrefilter, envReduce, environment grid, slot, 'outward' SH)

Design: [K6-core-renderer.md](../../dev/design/K6-core-renderer.md) §2.3, §3.3, §3.5 (now with a "K6.2b as built"
note), Q3, D34. Builds on [K6-2.md](./K6-2.md) ('frame' lighting without the environment) and [K6-1.md](./K6-1.md).

**Landed:** the host environment texture (equirect or cube `GPUTexture`) reduced on the GPU into a buffer every later
kernel binds as a uniform — no CPU readback, so the first frame has its environment lights; the dominant 'directional'
light in the next free transmittance slot; the environment grid; 'outward' SH irradiance; 'volume' lighting's
environment lights; the `./three` core backend passing `scene.environment`. CPU twins with fail-before mutations for
every new kernel.

## Design changes (please review)

1. **`envReduce` is one workgroup of 64**, not 256: compat devices allow 128 invocations, and the design's other kernels
   use 64. It reduces 2048 map and 8192 work texels; cost is per environment change only.
2. **The environment kernels have their own group 0** (`VdbFrameEnvParams`). The buffer `envReduce` writes as storage is
   group 0 binding 3 (uniform) of every other kernel; one dispatch cannot bind it both ways.
3. **The blur is the GGX roughness-0.4 lobe** (D(h)·n·l with n = v = r, three's PMREM filter), computed directly at his
   64 × 32 texel directions, not a separable cone blur: closer to his map and as cheap at this size.
4. **The work map is in map space**; the rotation applies in the blur and the SH projection. The exact-area box then
   needs no rotation, and a new rotation reruns only the blur and the reduce.
5. **Direction tables**: texel directions and solid angles of both maps, and the horizon ring's turns, are f32 tables in
   the WGSL. WGSL sin / cos are 2^-11 accurate; through the GGX lobe that was 0.5 % against the twin, and ring seeds
   moved cell boundaries across texels. Near-ties of the brightest texel and the nearest seed use a 1e-5 tolerance
   (V98) so f32 and f64 agree.
6. **`VdbFrameUniforms.environment`** (slot, first grid light, grid on, present): the CPU decides what it can (his slot
   rule needs only the mode, the scene slots and diffusion); grid / none / 'outward' and the slot light's presence are
   decided on the GPU from the buffer's count.
7. **`VDBFrameEnvironment.rotation` may be a reflection** (documented): three's flipY equirect and its WebGPU cube
   lookups are reflections of the core's conventions, so no `flipY` field was added.
8. **'volume' lighting's environment** (his inject pass: every light marched, or 'outward') landed here too; the Step 5
   environment scenes gate it.
9. **`./three` CUBE_UV fallback**: a PMREM atlas is drawn into a 1024 × 512 lat-long (≈ PMREM mip 0) through three's own
   sampler; at 256 × 128 point samples lost most of the sun (5× less energy).

## DIVERGES rows (provisional, from V95; source mjurczyk 75e7345 unless noted)

- V95 — K6.2b: the environment input is the host's equirect or cube texture, prefiltered by our kernels (an exact-area 128 × 64 work map, a GGX roughness-0.4 convolution into his 64 × 32 map), not three's PMREM sampled at roughness 0.4 (`FrameEnvironmentMapMaterial`, `FrameRenderer` env map).
- V96 — K6.2b: 'outward' environment irradiance is the SH L2 projection of the work map with the Ramamoorthi–Hanrahan cosine convolution (three's getShIrradianceAt constants), not PMREM roughness 1 × π (`FrameDensityMaterial`, `FrameInjectMaterial` environmentIrradiance). A/B: 47–48 dB, 1.5–1.9 % px > 4/255 on the 'outward' rows; K6.R judges.
- V97 — K6.2b: the environment lights ('directional', 'spherical-harmonics') are extracted on the GPU into a buffer the kernels bind as a uniform; no CPU readback, no first-frame 'outward' fallback; the slot light's direction never reaches the CPU (`frameEnvironment.js`, `FrameRenderer` readback).
- V98 — K6.2b: near-ties of the 'directional' extraction go to the first candidate (the brightest texel within 1e-5 relative of the maximum; a seed must be nearer by more than 1e-5); his takes the first exact maximum and the strictly nearer seed (`frameEnvironment.js` getDirectionalLights). The TSL path keeps his rule.
- V99 — K6.2b: the transmittance and environment grid redraw signatures use the environment buffer's version (the CPU never sees the lights) instead of the light directions and colours (`FrameRenderer` transmittanceUnchanged, renderEnvironmentGrid).
- V100 — K6.2b: the environment grid is an R³ 3D texture read with hardware trilinear, not a 2D slice atlas (`FrameEnvironmentGridMaterial`, `sampleTiles`); same clamps.
- V101 — K6.2b: the `./three` core backend passes `scene.environment`'s own texture (three's rotation with the flipY / cube reflection); a PMREM CUBE_UV atlas is drawn once into a 1024 × 512 lat-long at roughness 0.

## Traps added to CLAUDE.md

- WGSL sin / cos are 2^-11 accurate: direction-sensitive kernels need f32 tables (or tolerant twins).
- A buffer written as storage in one dispatch and bound as a uniform later in the same pass: the writer's layout must not
  include it.
- three's flipY equirect: GPU row 0 is the nadir; the cube lookups negate x.
- A PMREM atlas point-sampled into a coarse lat-long loses a small sun.
- k3-frame `?backend=core` reads the core's environment lights at once; TSL waits for its readback.
