## three r187 internals (B bucket) and their checks

`three/frame/three-internals.ts` documents each; a failing check throws `VDB_ENGINE_MISMATCH` (cached, rethrown) before
three is touched:
1. `new LightsNode().setLights([light]).setupLightsNode(builder)` returns the light's shared `AnalyticLightNode`, whose
   `disposeShadow()` drops the shadow node and bumps `version` (the lights cache key; receivers rebuild). Self-tested
   once per page on a throwaway `DirectionalLight` (two `LightsNode`s give one node; dispose + version) at the first
   `castShadows` update, before any filter goes on a light. Unit fail-before: a `setupLightsNode` that does not share the
   node, a `disposeShadow` that does not bump `version`: both throw.
2. `ShadowNode` / `PointShadowNode` call `shadow.filterNode(inputs)` with `{ depthTexture, shadowCoord | bd3D, … }`;
   checked at the call; the default filters are the `three/tsl` exports (checked present).
3. `renderer._nodes.nodeFrame.renderId` (also the collector's since K3.1) and `NodeUpdateType.RENDER`.
4. `Light.registerNode` (checked at the first `VDBFrameEmissionLight`; `prototype._lightNode` must be set).
5. `renderer._precompilationState` (present; passes skip while it is set, as three's `ShadowNode`).
6. `RendererUtils.resetRendererState` / `restoreRendererState`.

- Traps worth adding to CLAUDE.md: (1) SwiftShader rasterizes varyings (e.g. `positionWorld`) over a large triangle with
  ~1e-4 relative error: a texture read at a world position then moves by a few hundredths of a texel; subdivide receiver
  meshes in GPU twins and allow a position tolerance at sharp edges. (2) SwiftShader's division is ~12 bits (box-segment
  ends move by ~2e-4): a march whose sample lattice is anchored at those ends aliases sharp features (the explosion's
  flames) into several % per ray; twin such passes on smooth data and gate sharp data on aggregates. (3) His volume
  shadow filter goes on a light in the volume proxy's `onBeforeRender`, after the frame's lit meshes drew: shadows
  appear from the second render after `castShadows` turns on (harnesses render a few frames first; the oldest changed
  piece is always drawn, so the budget never delays the first maps).
