# Decisions

Settled calls. Each has a date, what was decided, and why. Change one only with a new entry that supersedes it;
strike the old one through and point at the new ID. **Proposed** rows are the coordinator's recommendation and
are not settled until the owner confirms them.

D1–D6 come from the v1 study. Their full text is in [docs/DECISIONS.md](docs/DECISIONS.md).

| D10 | 2026-11-18 | **GPU contract.** A kernel binds **one** storage buffer at a host-chosen slot (`{ group, firstBinding }`). That buffer holds a grid table plus any number of NanoVDB grid images, 16-byte aligned, with a versioned layout. WGSL is generated per feature set, so unused features compile out entirely, and every symbol is prefixed `vdb` (D17). No textures or samplers are required. | The pathtracer's measured constraints: Apple Chrome allows 10 storage buffers per stage and its kernels already use 8–9, and kernels are 41–55 KB of WGSL with compile time on watch. Several grids must fit one buffer, because bindless is not portable yet. bvh's `traceSlot` is the precedent. |
| D11 | 2026-11-18 | **Correctness and process rules adopted from the pathtracer.** Every WGSL entry point has a CPU twin used as the test oracle. Departures from a source go in DIVERGES.md. Competing approaches are built both ways behind a setting and A/B-tested. Timings that decide anything come from a quiet machine. | This repo already worked this way (657/657 and 22.5k-probe parity suites). Writing it down keeps agents in this repo and its consumers on one method. |
| ~~D12~~ | 2026-11-18 | ~~**Our core grows a general volume renderer.** mjurczyk's `FrameRenderer` (three-openvdb, MIT) is studied as a reference (R1), not ported wholesale. | D13 | 2026-11-18 | **Proposed:** `@pmndrs/vdb` code is licensed **MIT**. | D14 | 2026-11-18 | **Two formats, two jobs.** `.nvdb` is the exact format: sparse, full resolution, read and written. `.wvdb` (mjurczyk's spec v0.5) is supported for delivery, read and written by `./tools`. | Measured: at equal resolution our FpN beats `.wvdb` on size (474 vs 790 KiB on smoke). `.wvdb` still has coarse-first streaming and an existing user base. Supporting both is cheap (the spec is about 600 lines) and helps the joint-project pitch. |
| D15 | 2026-11-18 | The v1 plan ([docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md), phases 0–9) is frozen. Its unfinished items moved to the research track in PHASES.md (R2–R10). | v1 shipped phases 0–7 in CI scope. What remains needs hardware, native tools, or a decision that the new architecture changes. |
| D16 | 2026-11-18 | **Proposed:** PicoVDB is not adopted now. | D17 | 2026-11-18 | The project is **`@pmndrs/vdb`**, part of the pmndrs rendering initiative (the owner sits on its steering committee). One published package with subpaths: `.` (WebGPU core), `./tools` (CPU: parse, build, quantize, write), `./three`, and later `./babylon` and others. Private workspace packages are bundled at build. The repo moves to `pmndrs/vdb`. The v1 unscoped names are not published. Public symbols use `VDB` (classes) and `vdb` (functions, WGSL). | A scoped name avoids the clash with mjurczyk's `webvdb`/`openvdb` packages that the KumoVDB name was invented for, and matches `@pmndrs/bvh` and `@pmndrs/upscaler`. `@pmndrs/vdb` was free on npm (checked 2026-11-18). If mjurczyk joins, the joint project lives here. |
| D18 | 2026-11-18 | **Compete on rendering by porting.** mjurczyk's renderer (three-openvdb, MIT) is brought in as the basis of the `./three` renderer, re-based on the sparse core behind a volume-source interface (sparse pool or dense `.wvdb` levels). | D19 | 2026-11-18 | **Our phases first; the pathtracer after 0.1.** K0–K5 do not wait on or gate on pmndrs/pathtracer. | D20 | 2026-11-18 | **Layout:** one workspace package, `packages/vdb`, with `src/{core,tools,three}` joined by relative imports and built by a single `tsc` into `dist/{core,tools,three}`. | D21 | 2026-11-18 | **WGSL budgets come from measurement.** K1 gates on the per-profile budgets in [docs/design/K1-core.md](docs/design/K1-core.md) §3.6 (e.g. `read`/Float ≤ 17.5 KB min), enforced by a size snapshot in CI. | D22 | 2026-11-18 | **Majorants after 0.1.** The `majorant` WGSL profile and the brick-majorant builder move to P1 (pathtracer).
