# Divergences

Where we do something differently from a source, and why. Columns: what the source does, what we do, the kind of
reason (platform / perf / licence / simplification / bug-workaround / extension), and when to revisit. Seeded
from v1 on 2026-11-18; add new rows at the bottom.

| ID | Date | Source | What we do differently | Kind | Revisit when |

| V1 | 2026-07-11 | emcfarlane `pnanovdb.wgsl` @265e8d8 | Extended in place: Fp4/Fp8/Fp16/FpN decoders, typed dispatch, world↔index map, trilinear sampling, stats readers. Upstream lines are untouched; the diff log is in `vendor/VENDOR.md`. | extension | K1.4 splits the module into profiles |
| V2 | 2026-07-11 | `PNanoVDB.h` 32-bit address mode | 64-bit child offsets are read as their low 32 bits, as upstream does in 32-bit mode. | platform | A grid over 4 GiB |

| V14 | 2026-11-18 | `PNanoVDB.h` `LeafFnBase` (Fp leaf min/max/ave/stddev are u16 codes) | The vendored fork read those four slots as f32. | V15 | 2026-11-18 | c-blosc 1.21 `blosc_d` | The pure-TS Blosc decoder (K4.1 hardening) adds bounds checks c-blosc lacks, such as the block-start lower bound (`bstart >= 16 + 4·nblocks`), and rejects frames c-blosc would tolerate: `cbytes` must equal the on-disk byte count, and a memcpy'd frame must have `cbytes = nbytes + 16`. | V16 | 2026-11-18 | mjurczyk/openvdb `.wvdb` reader (`WVDBReader`, spec §14) | Our reader enforces spec §14 (sizes, indices, bounds, mask = page indices) and rejects files his reader accepts. | V17 | 2026-11-18 | mjurczyk `bake.js` / `sampleLevelSet` (level sets) | Level-set bakes follow the spec text (§8: inactive → ±b by sign, active → clamp to ±a) and point-sample. | V18 | 2026-11-18 | mjurczyk `bake.js` (header `meanDensity` / `nonZeroVoxels`) | We compute these from the values given to the writer (pre-quantization), like his current writer. | V19 | 2026-11-18 | mjurczyk CLI default zlib (Node `zlib`, level 9) | Default deflate is fflate `zlibSync` at level 9, so the browser stays free of `node:` imports. Pass `compress` (e.g. Node `deflateSync`) to match his CLI under the same Node. | platform | Byte identity with his CLI is needed in the browser |
| V20 | 2026-11-18 | mjurczyk `bake.js` resampling (point sampling only, §3.2.2) | `resampleGrid` (K4.4) defaults to a **box** prefilter (area-weighted mean over the target cell); `'point'` reproduces his samples exactly on his lattice, and `'trilinear'` is a third option. The output is a sparse NanoVDB grid, not a dense texture: only target voxels whose footprint touches an active source voxel are kept. | extension | The K4.5 A/B (rendered previews) picks a different default |
