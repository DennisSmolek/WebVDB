# Divergences

Where we do something differently from a source, and why. Columns: what the source does, what we do, the kind of
reason (platform / perf / licence / simplification / bug-workaround / extension), and when to revisit. Seeded
from v1 on 2026-11-18; add new rows at the bottom.

| ID | Date | Source | What we do differently | Kind | Revisit when |

| V16 | 2026-11-18 | mjurczyk/openvdb `.wvdb` reader (`WVDBReader`, spec §14) | Our reader enforces spec §14 (sizes, indices, bounds, mask = page indices) and rejects files his reader accepts. | V17 | 2026-11-18 | mjurczyk `bake.js` / `sampleLevelSet` (level sets) | Level-set bakes follow the spec text (§8: inactive → ±b by sign, active → clamp to ±a) and point-sample. | V18 | 2026-11-18 | mjurczyk `bake.js` (header `meanDensity` / `nonZeroVoxels`) | We compute these from the values given to the writer (pre-quantization), like his current writer. | V19 | 2026-11-18 | mjurczyk CLI default zlib (Node `zlib`, level 9) | Default deflate is fflate `zlibSync` at level 9, so the browser stays free of `node:` imports.
